You may enter customer details (such as name, mobile number, email, address and vehicle or property details) to prepare a quotation. This information is stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage. We do not operate a server that receives, stores or sells this information.

Information you enter is used only to calculate premiums, display and print quotations, and keep your saved quotations and settings on your device.

Information leaves your device only when you choose to share it — for example by tapping “Send on WhatsApp”, which opens WhatsApp with the quotation text, or by printing or saving a quotation.

Data stays on your device until you delete saved quotations in the app, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app.

Because data is stored on your device, please protect your device with a screen lock. The app’s Owner/User selector is a convenience feature and is not a security control.
