K-iNSURE update

Motor & Fire Premium Calculators: corrections and new features

Version 1.6.2 · 25 November 2026
✨Matching welcome screensNew in 1.6.2

Motor, Fire and Health now open with the same clean welcome card: K-CUBE logo, "Welcome to K-iNSURE", a yellow GET STARTED button, and "All rights reserved - Kota Kishore Kumar (Kota112457.uii.ms)".

🔧Commercial vehicles: electrical accessories correctedFixed in 1.6.1

For commercial vehicles (Taxi, Auto, Goods, Bus and others), the electrical accessories premium (4% of value) is now part of Own Damage, so the tariff discount and NCB apply to it, as in the insurer worksheet.

Example: Taxi, IDV ₹2,50,000, accessories ₹1,13,000, discount 70% — total now ₹19,232 (was ₹22,965). Private cars and two-wheelers are unchanged.

🩺Health Calculator is hereNew in 1.6.0

The Health Calculator now works, with four products: IHP (Individual Health), FMP (Family Medicare, floater or individual), STUMP (Super Top-Up) and Yuvaan.

Enter the PIN code (zone is found automatically), add family members with date of birth and sum insured, and tap Calculate. Share the summary on WhatsApp or as a PDF. Open it from the menu, the Dashboard card, or the phone home screen.

Health rates & logic: Health Dept. Team, Nagpur.

💼Incentives: Commission and Incentive tabsNew in 1.5.1

The Incentives page now has two tabs. Commission: Motor by state, plus Fire, Engineering and Marine Hull. Incentive: Fire and Engineering incentive with the total payout (commission + incentive), and the agents' additional commission.

Search Fire by IIB code or name, for example 1007 or hospital. Enter a net premium to see every amount in rupees.

💰Incentives: UIIC commission ratesNew in 1.5.0

The Incentives tab now shows the revised UIIC commission: Motor from 26.10.2026 and Fire & Engineering from 01.11.2026.

Choose the state where the vehicle is registered and each table shows just the commission that applies, with ▲ / ▼ against the earlier rate. Type a net premium to see the commission in rupees. On the phone, tap 💰 Incentives on the home screen.

🩺Health Calculator and Incentives tabsNew in 1.4.9

The Dashboard's left menu now has Health Calculator (below Fire Calculator) and Incentives (below Quotations). Both are marked Coming soon while they are being built.

🎨New look: Midnight, grey and sunflower yellowNew in 1.4.8

The home screen, Motor and Fire calculators now use a midnight-navy and sunflower-yellow design on a grey background, with matching text colours.

Only the colours changed: your K-CUBE logo, the layout, every calculation and the printed or shared quotation stay exactly the same.

🔌No Engine Protection for electric vehiclesNew in 1.4.7

Electric vehicles have no engine, so Engine Protection (Platinum), Engine Protection — Manual and Engine & Gearbox are no longer shown or charged for E-Private Car, E-Two Wheeler, EV-2W Passenger and E-PCV (Taxi).

This applies to every policy type: Comprehensive, Standalone OD and Bundled. Petrol, diesel and CNG vehicles are unchanged.

⚡Bundled policy for electric vehiclesNew in 1.4.6

New E-Private Cars and E-Two Wheelers now have the Bundled policy option, the same as Private Cars and Two Wheelers:

E-Private Car: BDL-1Yr OD + 3Yr TP, or BDL-3Yr OD + 3Yr TP.
E-Two Wheeler: BDL-1Yr OD + 5Yr TP, or BDL-5Yr OD + 5Yr TP.

Long-term Third Party for E-Two Wheelers is now 5 years (it was 3 years).

🔒Fire: "Permission Denied" pop-upNew in 1.4.5

In the Fire calculator, when someone who is not the Owner tries to change a rate or setting, a Permission Denied pop-up now appears over the calculator instead of a separate Conditions screen.

Tap OK to go back to the calculator (nothing entered is lost), or Owner Login to log in and make the change.

⛽Built-in CNG/LPG: commercial vehicles correctedMatches the UIIC worksheet

For commercial vehicles (lorry, goods auto, taxi, bus and others), the built-in CNG/LPG Own Damage loading is now 5% of Own Damage after the tariff discount. Private cars and two-wheelers stay at 2% of Basic Own Damage.

For Goods Auto and Goods Lorry, the ₹60 CNG/LPG liability is now taxed at 5% GST together with Basic TP, not at 18%.

Example: Goods Lorry, IDV ₹6,24,150, discount 45.1% — total now ₹27,002, same as the insurer.

🛞Tyre & Rim on Standalone ODPrivate cars

Private-car Tyre and Rim Protector can now be added to Standalone OD policies as well as one-year package policies (e.g. ₹2,000 for ₹50,000 cover).

₹Whole rupees everywhereMotor and Fire calculators

All amounts in the Motor and Fire calculators, quotations and WhatsApp messages are now shown in whole rupees, with paise rounded off. The amount in words no longer shows paise.

🏷️No Claim Bonus now includes Return to InvoiceMatches the insurer's quotation

NCB is now applied to Own Damage after discount, Nil Depreciation and Return to Invoice premium.

Example: Private car, IDV ₹21,68,451, discount 80%, NCB 20% — NCB now ₹6,618.11, same as the insurer.

🛡️CNG/LPG on Third Party Only₹60 CNG/LPG liability can now be added

With Third Party Only, the Coverages column now shows the CNG/LPG Kit and Built in CNG/LPG options, so the fixed ₹60 CNG/LPG TP liability is charged. Other add-ons stay hidden, because they need Own Damage cover.

Example: Taxi, Zone B, 1197 cc, 4 passengers — TP ₹12,287 + GST ₹2,211.66, same as the insurer.

✅Nil Depreciation: NCB now correctMatches the insurer's quotation

No Claim Bonus is now worked out on the OD premium including the Nil Depreciation premium, the same way the insurer calculates it.
