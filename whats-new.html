⚡E-PCV (Taxi): all add-on coversEvery Taxi add-on now available

E-PCV (Taxi) now offers Nil Depreciation, Return to Invoice, Consumables, NCB Protect, EV Protect, EMI Protect, Loss of Key, Personal Belongings, Platinum PA, Engine Protection, Roadside Assistance, Towing, Medical Expenses, Personal Effects, Road Tax Cover and Electrical Fittings.

CNG/LPG is not offered for electric vehicles (E-PCV, E-Private Car, E-Two Wheeler, EV-2W Passenger), because they run only on battery power.
